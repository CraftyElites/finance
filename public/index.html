Frequently Asked Questions

Is funding guaranteed?

No. We prepare you and connect you. Actual investment decisions rest with the investors.

How is my tier determined?

You self-select a tier on the pricing page based on how much capital you're seeking. Company (institutional) packages are assigned during underwriting instead.

What happens after I submit my application?

You're redirected into our WhatsApp group to send your details. Once we confirm them and receive your payment receipt for your slot, we accept your application and email you a one-time link to a short investment-readiness assessment — completing it unlocks your follow-up slot, also sent by email.

How long does review take?

Most individual applications are reviewed within 72 hours. Company applications go through underwriting and can take up to 5 business days.

Can I reapply if I'm rejected?

Yes — you're welcome to reapply in a future cohort once you've addressed the feedback in your rejection email.
