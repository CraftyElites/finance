Linking fees are only due once a qualified investor introduction is confirmed.

WHO WE ARE

More than capital —
a talent pipeline built for Africa.

Levyni Co Limited began as a talent marketplace connecting businesses with skilled developers, designers, and digital professionals across Africa. We saw a gap: young Africans learning valuable skills with no structured path to paid work, and businesses struggling to find reliable talent fast.

Levyni Connect — the capital introduction platform on this site — extends that same mission upward: helping investor-ready founders and registered companies access real capital, the same way we help early talent access real work.
